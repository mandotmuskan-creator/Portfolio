and roughly how it goes —

The way I work.

Neither of those projects started with a screen. They started with a question about who was actually going to use the thing.

  1. 01

    Ask the boring questions first

    How often does this person sign in? What do they already know? The unglamorous answers are where the interesting decisions come from.

  2. 02

    Map it before you draw it

    Journeys, flows and states on paper first. It is much cheaper to move a step than to redesign a screen.

  3. 03

    Clarity over decoration

    Hierarchy, then contrast, then everything else. If someone has to hunt for the one thing that matters, the layout has not earned its keep.

  4. 04

    Leave a system behind

    Components, variants, tokens and annotations — built to be handed over, not just admired.
