how the work actually goes —

Four things I keep coming back to

Clarity first

Hierarchy before decoration. If someone has to hunt for the one thing that matters, the layout hasn't earned its keep yet.

Systems, not screens

Components, variants and tokens, documented with their states. It's the difference between a redesign that lasts and one that drifts by spring.

Ask the boring questions

How often does this person log in? What do they already know? Most interesting design decisions come out of an unglamorous answer.

Warmth counts

Even enterprise software is used by someone having a day. A little softness in the colour, the copy and the spacing goes a long way.

the fine print —

I am, admittedly,
a bit of a klutz.

I have knocked a full coffee onto a printed moodboard the morning of a review. I have tripped over the one cable in an otherwise empty room. I once sent a file named final-FINAL-v3-actually.fig and meant it sincerely.

None of that ever made it into the work — if anything it's why I'm so stubborn about the systems. When you know you'll drop something eventually, you build the kind of structure that survives being dropped.
